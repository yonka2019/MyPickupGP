# Privacy Policy — FilterSMS

**Last updated:** 2026-09-18
**App:** FilterSMS (`com.yapps.filtersms`)
**Developer:** y-apps

## Short version

FilterSMS collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything it reads stays on your phone.

## No network access

The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission. Android therefore blocks it from making any network connection, so no data can be transmitted — no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no accounts.

## Permissions and why

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `READ_SMS` | Read your messages from the system SMS database to show them. |
| `RECEIVE_SMS` | Notice a new message so it can notify you, or stay silent if a mute rule matches. |
| `READ_CONTACTS` | Show the sender's name and photo instead of a bare number. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Show the new-message notification. |

The app is read-only: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and it never writes the system's read flag.

## What is stored on your device

Stored in the app's private storage, readable only by FilterSMS:

- Your mute rules, muted senders, theme and text-size choices.
- Per-sender "read up to" marks, used to show unread counts.
- A cache of your conversation list, including the latest message text, so the list appears instantly at startup.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. No backup of it is uploaded anywhere by the app.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose it:

- **Export chat as .txt** or **Export rules** — you pick the destination app (mail, Drive, notes). That app then holds the copy, under its own privacy policy.
- **Tapping a link** in a message opens your browser, which behaves as it normally does.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
